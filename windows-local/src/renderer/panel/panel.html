gemini key needed

the brain: reads your screen and explains it. google's free tier, no credit card.

how do i get one? (takes 1 minute)
  1. open aistudio.google.com/apikey
  2. sign in with any google account
  3. accept the terms if it asks, then click create api key
  4. copy the key, paste it above, hit test it, then save

heads up: on the free tier google may use what you send to improve its products, so close anything private before asking.

groq key optional

faster ears: turns your voice into text with whisper, and saves your gemini quota for answers.

how do i get one?
  1. open console.groq.com/keys and sign up (google login works)
  2. click create api key, give it any name
  3. copy it right away (groq only shows it once) and paste it above
  4. set ears below to groq, then save

nerd stuff

the 10 second version

  1. open your ppt, pdf or lecture notes
  2. hold ctrl + alt and ask, like "what does this diagram mean?"
  3. let go. it looks at your screen, talks you through it and builds a little explainer card on the whiteboard: a zoomed snapshot, key terms and a diagram

try saying

"explain this slide step by step"

"what's the difference between these two terms?"

"derive this formula on the whiteboard"

"give me a real life example of this"
